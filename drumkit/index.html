Start the metronome and play along.
Each hit is measured against the nearest grid line. Calibrate once per device: it measures the delay between the click you hear and your hits, so the numbers show your timing and not the phone's audio lag.
Every note is scored: green perfect, yellow on time, red ✕ missed, red × an extra hit. A loop's score counts perfect hits fully, on-time hits 80%, and extra hits against you. Timing uses the Training room's strictness and latency fix. Rudiments can be played on any pad. Flams and drags aren't included, because the kit can't tell a grace note from a separate hit.
